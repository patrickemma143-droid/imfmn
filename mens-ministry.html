Men’s Ministry

Growing in faith, brotherhood, and service.

MEN OF IMFM

Grow stronger in faith and walk together.

International Mission Fellowship MN, Inc. supports the spiritual growth of men through worship, biblical instruction, fellowship, and evangelism. These shared rhythms help men deepen their understanding of the Christian faith and live it out with purpose.

01 · WORSHIP

Worship together

Gather around Christ and grow in a shared life of worship.

02 · THE WORD

Learn Scripture

Biblical instruction helps men build a deeper understanding of faith.

03 · COMMUNITY

Build brotherhood

Fellowship and church events create space for meaningful relationships and mutual encouragement.
