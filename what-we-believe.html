What We Believe

Seven convictions we hold together.

These core convictions shape our worship, discipleship, and life together. We believe the Bible is God’s Word and that salvation and fellowship with God come through Jesus Christ.

01

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the complete Word of God.

  • The 66 books of the Old and New Testaments were inspired by the Spirit of God as originally written.
  • Scripture is our final authority in every matter of faith and conduct. 2 Timothy 3:16

02

God

We believe in one God, Creator of all: holy, sovereign, and eternal. He exists in three equal Persons—the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

03

Jesus Christ

We believe in the absolute and essential deity of our Lord Jesus Christ.

  • He eternally existed with the Father in glory before His incarnation and was active in creation.
  • He was born of a virgin, lived without sin, died as our substitute, and rose bodily from the dead.
  • He ascended triumphantly, ministers on our behalf, and will personally return.

04

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is fully God and is a Person.

  • He convicts the world concerning sin, righteousness, and judgment.
  • He regenerates, indwells, seals, sanctifies, illuminates, and comforts those who believe in Jesus Christ.
  • He baptizes believers, gives gifts as He wills, and fills those who yield to Him.

05

Humanity

We believe Adam was created by God in His image. Through disobedience, humanity fell from that state.

  • All people inherit spiritual depravity and defilement.
  • All are guilty before God and subject to physical and spiritual death.

06

Salvation

We believe salvation is by the sovereign grace of God.

  • Christ willingly suffered a death that atones for sin.
  • We are justified by faith alone in the all-sufficient sacrifice and resurrection of Jesus Christ.

07

The Resurrection and the Church

  • We believe in the resurrection of both the saved and the lost: the saved to the resurrection of life and the lost to the resurrection of judgment.
  • By the Holy Spirit’s indwelling, Christians are enabled to live godly lives.
  • All believers in the Lord Jesus Christ are spiritually united as His universal Church.
