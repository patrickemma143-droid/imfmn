A fellowship on mission.

International Mission Fellowship Minnesota is a community of believers commissioned to carry the Gospel across borders—language, culture, and circumstance. We gather to worship, to be discipled, and to be sent.

Whether you are new to faith, exploring, or have walked with Christ for decades, you belong here.
