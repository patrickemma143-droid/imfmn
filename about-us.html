About Us

A registered fellowship on mission in Minnesota.

We are a body of believers made up of people from different backgrounds and stories, joined together because of the gospel of Christ.

We are delighted you are here to learn about what God is doing at International Mission Fellowship MN.

Who we are

International Mission Fellowship MN is registered in Minnesota as a charity and church organization with a vision of proclaiming Christ to all, developing the whole person into practicing disciples who advance the kingdom of God until Christ returns.

Our mission

Our mission is to proclaim Christ, teach, nurture, holistically develop, and present mature and dynamic Christians who multiply themselves for further advancement of the kingdom of God.

What we believe

These seven convictions guide our life together.

  • BibleWe believe the Bible as the inspired, the only infallible, and authoritative Word of God.
  • GodWe believe in one God, who is eternally existent in three persons (a triune God): Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
  • Jesus ChristWe believe in the deity of the Lord Jesus Christ, His virgin birth, His sinless life, His miracles, His vicarious and atoning death through His shed blood, His bodily resurrection, His ascension to the right hand of the Father, and His return in person and in power and glory.
  • SalvationWe believe that for salvation of the lost and sinful people, regeneration by the Holy Spirit is absolutely essential.
  • Holy SpiritWe believe we are in the present ministry of the Holy Spirit by whose indwelling the Christian is enabled to live a godly life.
  • ResurrectionWe believe in the resurrection of both the saved and the lost; they that are saved into the resurrection of life and they that are lost unto the resurrection of damnation.
  • The ChurchWe believe in the spiritual unity of believers in the Lord Jesus Christ—His universal church.
