About Us

A registered fellowship on mission in Minnesota.

Welcome to the fellowship

A family of faith, joined by the gospel of Christ.

We are a body of believers from different backgrounds and stories, brought together by the gospel. We’re glad you’re here to learn about what God is doing at International Mission Fellowship MN and explore our programs for men, women, and children.

Who we are

A registered fellowship on mission in Minnesota.

International Mission Fellowship MN, Inc. (IMFM) is registered in Minnesota as a charity and church organization. Our vision is to proclaim Christ to all and develop the whole person into practicing disciples who advance the kingdom of God until Christ returns.

Our mission

Growing disciples who multiply.

Our mission is to proclaim Christ, teach, nurture, and holistically develop mature and dynamic Christians who multiply themselves for the further advancement of God’s kingdom.
