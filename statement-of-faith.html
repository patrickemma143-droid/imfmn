Statement of Faith

The beliefs of International Mission Fellowship MN.

Our confession

What we believe shapes how we worship and live.

These convictions express the shared faith of International Mission Fellowship MN.

01

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, only infallible, and authoritative Word of God.

02

God

We believe in one God, eternally existent in three Persons—the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

03

Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity of the Lord Jesus Christ, His virgin birth, His sinless life, His miracles, His vicarious and atoning death through His shed blood, His bodily resurrection, His ascension to the right hand of the Father, and His return in person and in power and glory.

04

Salvation

We believe that, for the salvation of lost and sinful people, regeneration by the Holy Spirit is absolutely essential.

05

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present ministry of the Holy Spirit, whose indwelling enables the Christian to live a godly life.

06

The Resurrection

We believe in the resurrection of both the saved and the lost: the saved to the resurrection of life, and the lost to the resurrection of judgment.

07

The Church

We believe in the spiritual unity of all believers in the Lord Jesus Christ—His universal Church.
