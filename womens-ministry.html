Women’s Ministry

Valued women, growing together in faith and service.

WOMEN OF IMFM

Every woman has a part in the life of the church.

International Mission Fellowship recognizes women as essential members of the church community. Their unique talents, strengths, and perspectives enrich the body of Christ. We seek to encourage women to grow, use their gifts, and work together for the good of the church and the wider community.

01 · BELONG

Find fellowship

Come together, build relationships, and strengthen unity across the church family.

02 · GROW

Develop your gifts

Discover and use your God-given strengths to encourage and serve others.

03 · SERVE

Make a difference

Collaborate in initiatives that bless the church and the wider community.
