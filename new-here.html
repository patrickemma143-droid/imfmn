We’re glad you found us.

We’re delighted you want to learn about what God is doing at International Mission Fellowship MN. Explore at your own pace, grab a cup of coffee, and find out more about our fellowship and programs for men, women, and children. If you have questions, we’d be glad to hear from you.

Who we are

A fellowship on mission.

International Mission Fellowship MN, Inc. is registered in Minnesota as a charity and church organization. Our vision is to proclaim Christ and develop practicing disciples who advance the kingdom of God until Christ returns.

Our mission

Growing and multiplying disciples.

We proclaim Christ, teach, nurture, and holistically develop mature and dynamic Christians who multiply themselves for the further advancement of God’s kingdom.
