Your first Sunday

What to expect.

01

What are services like?

Through music, prayer, and preaching, God’s Word is at the center of our gatherings. Each service includes prayer, preaching, and giving. Sermons are grounded in Scripture and aim to help us become more like Jesus Christ.

02

Where should I park and go?

There is parking near the church, including designated guest spaces near the front door. Greeters at the doors will be happy to help you find your way inside.

03

Is childcare available?

Clean, well-staffed nurseries for infants and toddlers are provided during church services. Sunday school for children is also available.

04

What should I wear?

We care more about you and your presence than what you wear. Come in what feels comfortable; members typically dress from business formal to business casual.
