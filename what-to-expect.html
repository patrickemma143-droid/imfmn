What To Expect

We look forward to meeting you.

Your first visit

We look forward to meeting you.

Welcome to International Mission Fellowship MN. We’ve gathered a few details to answer questions you may have before visiting for the first time.

01

A warm atmosphere

It’s important to us that you feel welcome. We hope you’ll feel relaxed and comfortable. Our music and worship are built around timeless Biblical themes, and you’ll be greeted when you arrive.

02

Family friendly

Whatever your family looks like, you’re welcome here. We want to serve the whole family. Care is available for infants and children during worship, where caring leaders provide spiritual training.

03

Expository sermons

From the pulpit, you’ll hear sound expository preaching. Each sermon focuses on the point of the Biblical text being addressed and its relevant theme for the season.

04

Biblical teaching

We place a high priority on preaching and teaching God’s Word. We gather to learn, be encouraged, and be challenged by Scripture. Beyond Sunday worship, there are other opportunities to grow, fellowship, and live out faith as a church family.

Ready to come?

We’ll be glad to welcome you.

Sunday worship is at 10:00 AM. Sunday school for children is also available.
